State-of-the-Facilities

Library

Our library is the intellectual heart of Lions P.U. College. With over 20,000 books, e-journals, newspapers and digital resources, it serves students from Higher Primary Schoolto P.U. College.

Science Laboratories

Four fully-equipped labs — Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Computer Science — enable students to learn through experimentation and discovery.

Auditorium

The 800-seat air-conditioned auditorium is the venue for annual days, cultural programs, career fairs, seminars and guest speaker events throughout the year.

Hostel

A supportive and secure on-campus residence fostering a close-knit student community with essential study spaces and daily conveniences. Designed for academic focus and personal growth, it offers a safe, immersive home away from home right in the heart of campus life.
