Welcome to Dr. Bhaskar Swadi Memorial Lions P.U. College

With a legacy of Sirsi Lions Education Society, serving the community since 1994, Dr Bhaskar Swadi Memorial Lions P.U. College, founded in 2023, Lions P.U. College is committed to nurturing confident, disciplined and future-ready young minds. Through quality education, dedicated mentorship, modern learning facilities and opportunities beyond the classroom, we empower students to discover their potential and build a strong foundation for their future.

Academic Sections

Higher Primary School

A strong foundation for lifelong learning, combining academic knowledge with creativity, curiosity, discipline and essential life skills.

High School

A focused learning environment that strengthens academic fundamentals while encouraging students to explore their interests, develop confidence and prepare for future opportunities.

P.U. College

A future-focused pre-university education offering Science and Commerce streams, with expert guidance, competitive exam preparation and pathways toward higher education and professional careers.
