Admission Process

1
Submit Application

Fill the online enquiry form or visit our admissions office to collect the application form.

2
Document Verification

Submit required documents: birth certificate, previous school TC, mark sheets and photographs.

3
Interaction / Assessment

An informal interaction session with the child and parents with our academic team.

4
Fee Payment

Pay the admission fee as per the fee structure. Scholarships available for meritorious students.

5
Welcome to Lions Family

Receive your admission confirmation letter and welcome kit. You're now part of Lions!
